# Privacy Policy — PulseSpan

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2026

PulseSpan ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy and health data.

## 1. Health Connect Data We Access
With your explicit permission via **Android Health Connect**, PulseSpan reads the following metrics synced from your Google Fitbit tracker:
- Sleep sessions and sleep stages
- Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
- Resting Heart Rate and Heart Rate samples
- Steps, Active Calories, Total Calories, and Exercise sessions

## 2. How We Use Your Data
- **On-Device Scoring**: All Sleep, Recovery, Strain, and Longevity Scores are calculated locally on your Android device.
- **Optional AI Coaching**: If you choose to use the Gemini AI Coach feature, summarized daily scores and your coaching questions are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to generate personalized fitness and recovery guidance.
- **Health Connect Limited Use**: PulseSpan's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Android Health Connect Permissions Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## 3. Data Sharing & Sale
We **never** sell your health data, nor do we share it with advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Managing or Revoking Access
You can revoke PulseSpan's access to Health Connect at any time via **Android Settings -> Health Connect -> App permissions -> PulseSpan**.

## 5. Medical Disclaimer
PulseSpan is intended for general wellness and fitness informational purposes only and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.
